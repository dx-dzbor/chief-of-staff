# INIT: set up your Personal OS

You are the agent helping the owner turn this public template into their own working context. Ask focused questions, draft useful content, and show consequential new records for review before writing them. This is a conversation; the owner may skip a section or provide a brain dump instead.

If you are the owner, open this folder in your agent and say: **"Read `INIT.md` and set me up."**

## Before the interview

Read `CONTEXT.md` and skim the project, stakeholder, and team `_TEMPLATE.md` files. Use `CLAUDE.md` for the default voice. Explain in two sentences that the OS keeps current priorities and relationships in markdown and that debriefs make future briefs better. Say that setup usually takes about 15 to 20 minutes and can be paused.

Ask one focused question at a time. Offer to infer from material the owner supplies. When a new profile, project, person, or decision record is ready, show its proposed contents and confirm them before writing; onboarding creates current state, not routine debrief notes. Write each confirmed section as you go. Do not send anything externally.

## 1. Identity

Ask for name, role, organization or context, and the work they own. Draft `me/index.md`, `me/profile.md`, `me/role.md`, and `me/role-expectations.md`. Give the index a short operating picture and current theme. Preserve its auto-generated markers.

## 2. Voice and expectations

Ask how they want to sound to their team, leaders, and a public audience; request one real short writing sample if they have one. Ask what outcome they want others to associate with their work and how they are evaluated. Fill `me/communications.md`, `me/personal-narrative.md`, and `me/expectations.md`.

Keep the baseline voice plain and direct: point first, evidence next, owner and action clear. Treat the sample as a stronger guide than generic style rules. Do not invent a "gold standard" quote and present it as the owner's own writing; label drafted examples until they approve them.

## 3. Direction and open decisions

Ask for the one outcome they are driving end to end, what done looks like, the live blocker, and the next move. Ask for this week's few outcomes, longer-term goals, and standing leadership guidance. Fill `me/current-drive.md`, `me/weekly-goals.md`, `me/long_term_goals.md`, and `me/exec_steer.md`. Set `week_of` to the current Monday.

Ask whether there are any already-made decisions with a follow-up or checkpoint. If so, show the exact proposed entries and, after confirmation, add them to `me/decisions.md` with a source of `onboarding: owner` and today's capture date. Keep the original decision date if the owner knows it; do not guess it. Leave `me/waiting-on.md` and `me/log.md` empty unless the owner gives an actual item to record.

## 4. Projects

Start with the most important one to five active projects. For each, ask its purpose, current state, priority, key people, blocker, next step, and tracking level (`full`, `selective`, or `passive`). Use `me/projects/_TEMPLATE.md` and add natural aliases for debrief routing. Connector fields can stay empty.

## 5. Stakeholders

For each important stakeholder, ask their role, connected projects, main concern, decision style, and any open ask. Use the stakeholder template under `me/stakeholders/top/` or `other/`. Add aliases and keep observed facts separate from the owner's interpretations.

## 6. Team

For each direct report or close collaborator, ask what they own, strengths, aspirations, and any attributed feedback. Use `me/team/_TEMPLATE.md`, set the manager slug, and add aliases. Skip this section if the owner has no team.

## 7. Status audience

Ask who should normally receive a weekly status draft and put those stakeholder slugs in `me/updates/config.md`. A draft is never sent automatically.

## Finish

Refresh the auto-generated region of `me/index.md`. Report how many real project, stakeholder, and team files were created and what remains unfinished. Once real files replace the fictional `EXAMPLE-*.md` files, offer to remove those examples; keep the templates. Check for remaining `[PLACEHOLDER]`, `[YOUR_`, and `TODO` markers in active files.

Give the owner the next three actions: run `/brief`, paste a dump into `/debrief` tonight, and run `/os-review` with `/weekly-status` at week's end. Mention `/eli5 <topic>` for a diagram-led explanation. After a few days, tune the skill instructions, project coverage, and aliases based on actual use rather than adding rules in advance.
