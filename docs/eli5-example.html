How a decision stays visible

A decision should not disappear into yesterday's notes. This system keeps the decision and its next checkpoint where tomorrow's brief can find them.

One decision, four stops

  1. The owner mentions the decision in a debrief. The exact words are saved.
  2. The agent asks before changing the current decision record.
  3. After approval, it records the decision and any owner or checkpoint.
  4. The next brief brings the checkpoint back when action is due.

Think of a tracked parcel

The decision is the parcel. Its ID and checkpoint make it easy to find until it arrives.

Where that picture breaks

A decision can change or be reversed. The record must show that change; it is not an automatic delivery system.

What this is based on

The workflow comes from the repository's context contract and debrief skill. Project Aurora, Riley, and the pilot decision are fictional examples, not real events.
